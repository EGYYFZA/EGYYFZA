## Research and data

[HoaxShield](https://github.com/EGYYFZA/multimodal-cnn-ocr) combines CNN classification,
OCR and reference-article matching in a local research prototype.
[Kompas News Collector](https://github.com/EGYYFZA/scrape-kompas) and
[TurnBackHoax Collector](https://github.com/EGYYFZA/scrape-turnbackhoax) support work
with Indonesian news datasets. Predictions and collected content require
independent review; project READMEs describe the boundaries.
